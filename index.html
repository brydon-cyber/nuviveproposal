Introduction

Dad, three years ago when we started this NuVive journey, the plan was simple: help build NuVive's business systems and get the Driven Operating System in place. Once that was built, I'd scale back to a weekly call and quarterly strategy sessions.

The scope has obviously grown past that over time. I'm proud of what we've built, and Mina and I are so grateful for how NuVive has helped us make it financially. But with Strive to Be and the King will come taking off and requiring more of me, I am needing to re-align my involvement in NuVive. To be honest, with the load I'm taking on, there is no way I can keep on engaging with NuVive the way that I currently am. I've even thought about taking a 6-month hiatus until the STB contract is done, but I don't think that is the best option. So what follows are some options I think could work. I'm eager to hear your feedback and get this locked in ASAP as my STB contract officially starts on Aug 10.

To be super clear, this isn't me stepping away from what I told you I'd do, although I've wondered if I'd have to given where my life is going. But I've decided I don't want to do that. What this is is me realigning back to what we agreed to at the start, because I want to finish the job and because I believe this path serves NuVive, you, and me best long term. So, read through and let's get on the same page ASAP. I'm excited about this. It's going to be great!

The Why

This isn't just about hours. It's part of a bigger overhaul of my personal and professional life.

Time. I'm buying back my time through AI, systems, and delegation, everywhere in my life, not just NuVive. Mina needs more of me than I'm giving her. Daven is only this age once, and I feel like I'm often trading his childhood for hours I could've automated or handed off.

Health. Yours and mine both need to stay a priority. We're doing well here, but you've been falling asleep in a lot of our sessions lately, and that tells me something isn't sustainable in how we're both operating.

Scaling. NuVive can't scale while it depends on my hours, and I can't scale Kwc, Strive to Be, and our family if I keep trying to be everywhere. I think the key to scaling is better systems, not more hours.

Selling. Whether or not you sell NuVive or pass it off to management, a business that runs on systems instead of me is worth more, and easier to hand off when that day comes.

Our relationship. I know you're worried scaling back NuVive hours means less time together. Honestly, I believe it's the opposite. Because we already spend 10+ hours a week on NuVive, it's harder for me to call or FaceTime just to connect because in my head and Mina's, we've already "seen" each other. Work hours also carry more stress than personal time. I'd rather scale back the work time and scale up the personal time. I think it's better for our relationship and even for your relationship with Mina and Daven.

Focus. Under the current setup, you haven't been getting my passion or my best. I often feel like an expensive data inputter, doing work that doesn't need my skill set. Once this realigns, you'll get a different Brydon: more focused, more present, and more useful to the clinic.

What My Role Becomes

Going forward, I believe my role needs to shift from helping operate NuVive to solely implementing the checklist that finishes the Driven Operating System build. That should be the whole job. For the good of NuVive, I should be removed from day to day operations.

Here's the honest picture of where I am now, and what I think needs to shift so I'm used at my highest leverage. Probably 50% of my NuVive time is "work in"/operational work. I'm leaned on for a lot of strategic decisions and staffing issues. I'm not the team's leader or point of contact on most things, but I'm in nearly every thread. I'm more of a fractional operator than a consultant/implementor as originally intended. Right now I'm fielding:

  • Tech questions
  • Vendor questions
  • "How do I answer this email" questions
  • A lot of staffing and personnel issues
  • Building the weekly team training agenda
  • Attending the weekly team training
  • 90-day training follow-ups and onboarding
  • Updating KPIs and financials, which the team should own
  • Being involved in the hiring process

None of that is where my time is most valuable to NuVive. I believe I need to move back into the original consultant/implementor role and let the team own the list I just enumerated.

Our sessions need to be tightly focused and entirely "work on" the business, never "work in" it. If day to day work needs my input, it should happen outside our sessions, not during them. I'll prepare every agenda and drive the results myself. Sharper time together actually buys you more room for the "work in" tasks that never get done, the ones that keep bleeding into our sessions now.

This Takes All of Us

I can't complete the deliverables myself, and NuVive wouldn't be served if I did. Part of finishing well means training Lisa and Brittney to take on more, so the office has real capacity beyond me. The team needs to own pieces of the checklist (below) directly.

That includes you too, Dad. Some of this is homework on your end between sessions. If "work on" only moves when I'm pushing it, we haven't built anything that lasts.

The Finish Line

"Done" means that every item on the checklist below is implemented and routinized. Not every system perfected, just the core framework, the rhythms, the data tools, the culture pieces, the structure, so you and the team can build on it without me embedded in it. Once it's complete, my role settles permanently into the weekly and quarterly cadence described in Life After Done.

Path to the Finish Line

Choose one. Proposed start date: August 11, 2026. There are three ways to get to the finish line. Full timelines for each are in Exhibit A, Exhibit B, and Exhibit C below.

Option A: The 90-Day Sprint

August 11 to November 30, 2026  ·  8 to 10 hours a week
  • Week one: we finalize the checklist together, then sprint every item to done
  • Hard end date, no drift
  • Fastest route to stabilizing NuVive

Honest trade-off: this is the tightest timeline of the three options. To be honest, I don't think it's really feasible with my Strive to Be and Kwc commitments but I'm including it here for discussion.

Option B: The 6-Month Build

August 2026 to March 2027  ·  4 to 6 hours a week
  • I operate as consultant and coach from day one, not operator, even while we build
  • Same checklist, same finish line, longer runway
  • Includes finally solving the meeting cadence problem for good: restructuring so 7am meetings are no longer needed (except for J1 calls)

Honest trade-off: six months is a longer runway and more feasible during Q3 and Q4 while STB and Kwc pull hard on me. But this only works if we both treat the finish line as fixed, not soft.

Option C: The 9-Month Build

August 2026 to June 2027  ·  3 to 4 hours a week
  • The lightest weekly lift of the three, leaning hardest on the team and on Lisa's growing capacity
  • Same checklist, same finish line, the longest runway
  • Built-in milestone checkpoints every few weeks, not just a longer deadline

Honest trade-off: nine months is the longest runway, and the risk is exactly what happened before, scope quietly drifting instead of finishing. Only works with hard milestones along the way, not just good intentions.

My Honest Read

Given my actual Q3 and Q4 load with Strive to Be intensifying and Kwc's Project Reset going live, Option A is nearly impossible. It would mean no margin anywhere else in my life for three months and I'm not confident we could even finish all of the deliverables in that timeline.

Option B gives real margin, but only works if we both treat the finish line as fixed, not soft.

Option C gives the most margin, but it's also most likely to drift the way we already have. If we go that route, we need real milestone checkpoints, not just a longer runway and good intentions.

I would propose Option B or Option C.

How This Is a Gain, Not a Loss

I know this can read as me pulling back. I don't see it that way, and here's why I think it's a gain for both of us.

  • You get a better version of me. Focused, present, and bringing my best focus to each session.
  • NuVive becomes worth more. A business that runs on systems instead of running on me is stronger, easier to scale, and easier to hand off or sell whenever that day comes.
  • The team grows. Lisa, Brittney and any other team members who come on take on real ownership instead of everything routing through me, which means more capacity for NuVive even when I'm not in the room.
  • We strengthen our relationship. Less time in the stress of work means more relational bandwidth for personal time with me, Mina, and Daven.
  • I get to build sustainably. No more dropping balls trying to hold up commitments I can't humanly carry alongside Strive to Be and Kwc.
  • More "work in" time. You get more time to get the critical "work in" tasks done that often consume our time together.
  • More time to sharpen the saw. You get more time to optimize your personal operating system and sharpen the saw so you are a more effective leader in the business.
  • More room for you to lead. I know you don't feel this, but I've noticed that when you are given the space to lead and make decisions fast, you thrive. I am good at giving counsel but in reality I'm a leader and sometimes we unintentionally compete and my presence ends up slowing you down. Re-aligning my role will allow you to establish yourself more firmly as the respected and decisive leader of NuVive.

Closing

Dad, I am not leaving you in the lurch. I want to finish what I told you I'd build, and finish it well. This isn't a new proposal, it's a return to what we agreed to at the start, because I believe that serves NuVive, you, and me best long term.

Let's talk it through together and decide the best path forward.

Love you so much,

Brydon
